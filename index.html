Mission & Identity

Forging the Next Generation of Cyber Defenders

CYBER CUJ is the official student-led cybersecurity organization of Central University of Jammu. We cultivate practical ethical hacking talent, security awareness, and vulnerability research.

"We believe true security is not achieved by erecting walls in ignorance, but by thoroughly understanding the exploit to architect impervious defenses."

Established at the Department of Computer Science & Information Technology, Central University of Jammu, CYBER CUJ provides students with real-world infrastructure to experiment, dissect vulnerabilities, and develop defensive strategies.

Through rigorous hands-on laboratory sessions, CTF war-games, red-vs-blue simulations, and awareness outreach across Jammu & Kashmir, our members bridge academic computer science theory with industrial offensive and defensive cybersecurity practices.

Hands-on Sandbox Labs

Isolated internal ranges and virtual machines simulating real-world enterprise infrastructure and Active Directory exploits.

Ethical Hacking Code

Strict adherence to responsible disclosure frameworks, legal boundary guidelines, and academic research integrity.

CTF Competitive Force

Regular intra-university sprints and organized representation in prestigious national & global CTF competitions.

Campus & State Awareness

Collaborating with state cyber cells to deliver digital literacy, fraud prevention, and device hardening workshops.

Core Disciplines

Our Technical Specializations

CYBER CUJ is structured into 8 specialized operational divisions. Members receive hands-on training and conduct real-world security experiments in each domain.

DOM-01

Ethical Hacking

Red team strategies, offensive exploit crafting, system privilege escalation, and testing security controls against advanced persistent threat (APT) tactics.

Metasploit Linux PrivEsc Cobalt Strike Shellcoding
DOM-02

Penetration Testing

Methodical vulnerability assessments, network perimeter pivoting, Active Directory exploitation, and generating professional remediation reports.

Nmap Nessus BloodHound OWASP Testing
DOM-03

Capture The Flag (CTF)

Designing and solving complex Jeopardy-style and Attack-Defense challenges. Fostering out-of-the-box analytical thinking under timed tournament pressure.

CTFd Binary Pwn Web Exploits Speed Hacking
DOM-04

Digital Forensics & IR

Extracting digital evidence, analyzing volatile memory dumps, tracing malware persistence artifacts, and reconstructing cyber crime timelines.

Volatility 3 Autopsy FTK Imager Timeline Analysis
DOM-05

Web Application Security

Uncovering server-side and client-side flaws: SQL Injection, Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), SSRF, Broken Access Controls, and JWT exploitation.

Burp Suite Pro OWASP Top 10 SQLMap API Security
DOM-06

Network Security & Sniffing

Deep packet inspection, MITM analysis, firewall configuration, IDS/IPS rulesets, and securing campus communication architectures.

Wireshark Snort IDS Zeek TCP/IP Internals
DOM-07

Applied Cryptography

Analyzing symmetric & asymmetric ciphers, breaking flawed crypto implementations (RSA, AES, ECC), mathematical attacks, and hash cracking.

Hashcat SageMath CyberChef RSA / ECC Attacks
DOM-08

OSINT & Threat Intelligence

Open-source intelligence gathering, geolocation verification, breach data analysis, dark web monitoring, and digital footprint profiling.

Maltego SpiderFoot Shodan Geo-Recon
